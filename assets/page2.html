pop-up catering

You’ll often find Cook&Curry popping up at bars, festivals, and community spaces, serving Caribbean flavours straight from our famous gazebo.

We bring people together through the joy of street food—vibrant, gluten-free, halal dishes made with love and perfect for sharing. Whether it’s a lively festival, an intimate gathering, or a buzzing bar night, we create a culinary experience that feels warm, fun, and unforgettable.

If you’d like us to pop up at your event, we’d love to hear from you!
